## Task brief · embedding

**Problem**

The Framework's embedding row serves English-only granite-embedding-english-r2; ~/docs holds 13
languages. multilingual-e5-large changes what callers depend on: 1,024 dimensions, a 512-token
limit, and caller-added `query: ` / `passage: ` prefixes. It costs about four times granite's
compute per token, beside an audio row that measured 5.84 against its floor of 5 at 34 req/s. So
the task measures that the served model retrieves as published, that audio holds beside it, and at
what rate. Nothing stored needs re-embedding.

**Behaviors**
1. A separate mise environment pins Python, GuideLLM 0.8.1 and MTEB 2.27.22, each exact.
   `mise run check` never installs them and still reads no network.
2. `mise run currency` reports a trailing bench pin as it does every other pin, and exits 0 when
   all are current.
3. ai-hosting carries spike-model-layout's GuideLLM scenarios: the embedding sweep (1, 4, 16, 32,
   64, 128 streams, 30 s each), embedding at a constant rate given per run for 240 s, and audio at
   4 streams for 240 s. Each embedding input is 510 synthetic tokens. The check fails when a
   scenario doesn't parse, or when an embedding scenario's input tokens plus two special tokens
   exceed the embedding row's context in the strix-halo profile.
4. One command in the bench environment runs MTEB's BelebeleRetrieval against a row's
   `/v1/embeddings`. It adds a query prefix and a passage prefix, both given as arguments, and runs
   the subsets given, or by default the 37 subsets for ~/docs' 13 languages (each alone and paired
   with English). It writes MTEB's result for each subset.
5. The strix-halo profile's embedding row serves `intfloat/multilingual-e5-large` with `dims`
   1,024 and `context` and `card_context` 512. It keeps the pooling runner with 65,536 batched
   tokens a step and takes its cap and fraction from behaviors 8 and 9. `measured_vllm` is v0.34.0,
   and its source is publisher `Microsoft`, origin `United States`, licence `MIT`, and the card URL.
   Each reason cites a cell in the findings document. The row passes `hosting profile validate`,
   and `hosting profile render embedding` prints e5's command with `--max-model-len 512`.
6. On the Framework, `hosting engine update embedding` replaces the row. After it:
   - `hosting engine status` exits 0;
   - `hosting engine models` shows the row serving under `embedding`;
   - `hosting engine update` with no alias changes nothing;
   - `hosting model list` shows e5 served by `embedding`;
   - `hosting model prune` lists granite, and once `--yes` removes it, lists nothing.
7. Probes from the laptop: `/v1/models` answers `embedding`; a vector has 1,024 components and its
   norm is recorded; a 510-token input embeds; an input one token over the context is refused. The
   row takes the audio image's MIOpen setting only if the long-input probe fails.
8. The embedding sweep records the knee and the plateau. The cap is the highest level, at most 128,
   that keeps the plateau with no error or preemption. cap × 512 stays within the batched tokens,
   and the row meets embedding's floor of 4,300 prompt tok/s.
9. `hosting engine memory` is read with both Framework rows idle. Fraction 0.03 stays if e5 starts
   and serves within it; otherwise it rises to the smallest fraction that does. The free GTT is
   recorded either way.
10. The served e5 scores within 0.02 nDCG@10 of its published BelebeleRetrieval score on every one
    of the 37 subsets. A miss is first diagnosed as a serving fault (prefixes, truncation, pooling,
    normalization), and counts against the model only once those are ruled out.
11. Audio alone: the audio row at 4 streams for 240 s with the embedding row idle. Its RTFx median
    is recorded.
12. Audio beside e5: audio at 4 streams beside embedding at 34 req/s for 240 s holds an RTFx
    median of at least 0.89 × its alone median (escalation 2), and embedding keeps its rate with no
    errors. If audio misses, the cell reruns at rates descending from 34 req/s in steps
    of at most 4 req/s. The highest rate that holds becomes the limit, never below the rate that
    meets embedding's 4,300 prompt tok/s floor (8.5 req/s at 510 tokens). If no rate holds, e5
    fails (behavior 17).
13. The embedding row's load target reads "16 streams, at most <rate>", from behaviors 8 and 12.
    The audio row's reasons that cite the granite pair cite the e5 pair instead, and the gateway
    note's embedding bucket states the rate.
14. The findings document records each cell's command, time, figures and tool versions. It also
    records the tokens per rune e5's tokenizer gives on ~/docs for each language, and the rune
    bound that fits 512 tokens, for docinfer's owner.
15. The Foundry overflow note re-reads e5's catalog entry: each sourced fact confirmed or
    corrected, with the date read. Nothing is deployed.
16. The Framework runbook's embedding section states the caller's `query: ` and `passage: `
    prefixes, and the 512-token limit with its refusal. The engines note records the long-input
    probe's outcome. The host-classes note cites this replacement as the pattern for replacing a
    model.
17. If e5 fails behavior 7, 10 or 12, EmbeddingGemma 2 is checked next: that vLLM v0.34.0 serves
    it on the Framework, its dimensions and context, and that Azure Foundry offers it. If those
    pass, it goes through behaviors 5 to 13 in e5's place. A Chinese-origin candidate is
    considered only if both fail; it goes to the architect first, with its `origin_reason`
    recorded.
18. After the merge, the Framework's clone is on main and behavior 6 holds again.

**Test seams**
- The check (behaviors 1, 3, 5): the scenario lint's shell test, the profile validator, and the
  render fixture.
- `mise run currency` (behavior 2).
- Behaviors 4, 6–13 and 17: `hosting` commands over ssh on the Framework, and the GuideLLM and
  MTEB result files, quoted in the findings document and the session brief's Verify.
- Behaviors 14–16: a read, plus the check's link resolution.

**Slices**

Both repositories are current, so there are no upgrade slices.
1. **The bench environment and scenarios** (behaviors 1–3). The check passes with the lint's
   tests, and currency exits 0.
2. **The retrieval runner** (behavior 4). Demo: one English subset run against the current granite
   row, with empty prefixes.
3. **e5 on the Framework's embedding row** (behaviors 5–9): the profile and render fixture, then
   `engine update`, the probes, the sweep, the memory read, and pruning granite.
4. **Retrieval across ~/docs' languages** (behavior 10): the 37 subsets against their published
   scores.
5. **Audio beside e5, and the rate** (behaviors 11–13): the alone cell, the pair cell or cells, and
   the profile's load target.
6. **The records** (behaviors 14–16): tokens per rune, the Foundry re-read, the runbook and the
   notes.

Behavior 17 adds a slice only if e5 fails; it repeats slices 3–5 for EmbeddingGemma 2.

**Out of scope**
- docinfer's chunk bound and prefixes, and go-ai's quick-starts: Pending edits.
- Foundry's deployment, the vector-parity cell, the standing layout cell: acceptance.
- The gateway's bucket config: gateway.
- A `hosting` measuring verb and the measuring convention: upgrades.
- Re-measuring the audio row's KV cache and context.
- Changing the vLLM pin.

**Door**

Two-way. The profile reverts with `profile set`, `model pull` and `engine update`, and granite can
be pulled again. Nothing goes to Azure, and ai-hosting tags no releases.

## Decisions

- plan: The `architecture` overlap with v1.ai.processing and harness.optimization stays, as the intake accepted (PR #174 decision 2).
- secrets plan: The gateway task replaces the spike gateway's key, `AI_GATEWAY_TOKEN` in go-ai's and
  docinfer's `fnox.toml`, with keys it issues.
- secrets: Enrolled the laptop and gb10-2, gb10-2 in ai-hosting alone; enrolling gb10-1 and the
  Framework is left to a later task.
- hosts plan: Any change to the Framework's LUKS unlock at the console goes to the backlog.
- hosts plan: Shell tests are plain-bash scripts run by the check; bats-core rejected: it adds a
  tool, and claude-plugins' plain-bash tests are the workspace precedent.
- migrate plan: the spike gateway is retired in migrate. Callers use the rows directly until the `gateway` task, whose handover starts from an empty gb10-2. Rejected: folding in `gateway`, which has six open points and depends on `embedding`; and a temporary gateway.
- migrate plan: `cleanup` is folded into migrate. It stays in `order` and Tasks, and is checked off at migrate's SHIP.
- migrate plan: the laptop's `~/experiments` clones stay. "Workstation" means the three hosts.

- embedding plan: The task touches ai-hosting only. architecture never names the embedding model,
  the layout, or its rate.
- embedding plan: Retrieval passes when the served model is within 0.02 nDCG@10 of its published
  BelebeleRetrieval score on each of the 37 subsets for ~/docs' 13 languages; a miss is first
  diagnosed as a serving fault.
- embedding plan: "Doesn't degrade audio" means audio's RTFx median at 4 streams beside embedding
  at the admitted rate, for 240 s, holds ≥ 0.89 × its alone median measured just before on the same
  path (granite's drop at 34 req/s). Measured from the laptop over the Tailscale relay; the floor
  of 5 is judged by acceptance. A miss lowers the limit, never below embedding's 4,300 tok/s floor.
- embedding plan: Measurements run from the laptop with GuideLLM and MTEB, in a mise environment
  the check never installs; the scenarios and findings live in ai-hosting, with no `hosting` verb.
  The upgrades task builds the repeatable convention.
- embedding plan: Foundry's deployment of the embedding model belongs to acceptance; this task
  only re-reads the catalog.
- embedding plan: Fallback order: multilingual-e5-large, then EmbeddingGemma 2 (if vLLM v0.34.0
  serves it and Foundry offers it), then a Chinese-origin candidate with its reason recorded.
  snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0 is excluded on origin: it is built on BAAI's bge-m3-retromae.
- embedding plan: e5's prefixes are the callers' job: the model declares no prompts that vLLM's
  `input_type` or TEI's `prompt_name` could read.
