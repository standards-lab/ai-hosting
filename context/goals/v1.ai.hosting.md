## Task brief · vision-image-tokens

**Problem**

The vision row gives each image Gemma 4's default 280 soft tokens, so dense pages lose their text and
the model loops: 95 CFR pages in docinfer's run. A client can't raise the budget per request
(400). Only the row can raise it, and the capstone's vision work reads the same pages.

**Behaviors**
1. The vision row serves with an image budget of 1120 soft tokens, set in its profile as an engine
   flag. Its alias, model, port, and context are unchanged, and the profile validates.
2. Two probes, without load, measure one image's prompt tokens at 280 (today), 560, and 1120: a
   1240×1754 page and a 3705×4934 page, one request each.
3. ai-hosting's bench has a vision capacity scenario, the spike's requests (one 1440p JPEG, 200 text
   tokens, 100 output tokens) at 4, 8, and 16 streams, 90 s a level. The bench environment
   installs GuideLLM's vision extra.
4. A preliminary capacity run at 560 and at 1120 on gb10-2, the vision row alone at its cap of 16,
   reads each level's latency median against vision's ≤10 s floor. A findings page records both
   runs with the probes, as audio's and embedding's do.
5. The row's cap, queue limit, and load target hold, or follow what the runs show: their reasons
   cite the new measurement. A cap the runs push below 16 is an escalation, not a silent change.
6. The goal record keeps one Decision: `acceptance` re-measures the vision row at 1120 in the
   standing layout cell.
7. A message to v1.ai.inference's agent, drafted for the architect to relay: the flag and value,
   the probe tokens, the capacity findings at both budgets, and any capacity change.

**Test seams**
- `hosting profile validate`, and the check, over the gb10 profile.
- The probes and runs on gb10-2 through `hosting engine update vision`.

**Slices**
1. Bench: the vision scenario and GuideLLM's vision extra. Check passes.
2. Measure at 560: the row at 560 from the branch on gb10-2, probes, capacity run, findings page.
3. Measure at 1120: the row at 1120, probes, capacity run. Findings updated, flags_reason and any
   capacity reasons cite them.
4. The goal record's Decision and the drafted message.

**Out of scope**

A per-request budget through the gateway, docinfer's re-probe and re-sweep, quality scoring of
the CFR pages, the full layout cell (`acceptance`), and the locked `upgrades` task.

**Door**

Two-way: one profile flag. Restarting gb10-2's vision row reaches outside the repository. The
architect authorizes it here, since docinfer is waiting on it, and docinfer tells the architect
before its own load.

**Addendum (audio, architect at BRIEF) · Problem**

The audio row's cap of 8 and fraction of 0.18 date from sharing its GPU, set by a per-request
speed floor on short clips. Alone on framework, under the architect's rule (accuracy first, then
the most clips processed over time), it is likely under-used, and its limits are unknown: the
longest clip it transcribes accurately and its throughput across clip lengths. It is tuned as a
general audio endpoint; clients such as docinfer shape their requests to what it proves.

8. A clip-length probe, without load: real speech (LibriSpeech clips joined) of about 10, 30, 60,
   and 120 s, one request each, recording prompt tokens per second of audio, the longest clip
   transcribed in full, and each failure mode (cut short, looping, refused, wrong language) with
   the length where it starts. A failure past the context is a finding, not an escalation.
9. Bench audio capacity scenarios at two clip lengths, short (about 5–10 s) and 30 s windows,
   plus the longest passing clip when past 30 s; stream levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, and 32, 90 s a
   level, enough rows that no level runs out, and `max_tokens` sized to a full transcript (a bench
   setting, not advice to clients). `levels.jq`'s audio purpose reports req/s beside RTFx.
10. A preliminary sweep on framework, the row alone at a raised fraction and cap 32, its KV cache
    holding 32 requests of 8,192; the host stays above its class's memory floor, MemAvailable
    recorded. Findings go into docs/findings/audio.md as new cells.
11. The row is set from the sweep: cap at the most clips over time across the lengths, each
    length's peak recorded, following the longer clips where peaks differ; fraction the smallest
    holding cap × 8,192; queue limit deeper than twice the cap with `queue_limit_reason`; load
    target the cap's level; reasons citing the new cells; RTFx reported, not a floor; context
    8,192. Redeployed on framework and probed.
12. The report to v1.ai.inference's agent covers both rows: for audio the longest accurate clip,
    tokens a second, throughput and latency by length and concurrency, cap and queue limit, and
    the failure modes. It recommends no client setting; docinfer shapes its own. The goal record's
    Decisions and pending edits cover audio as they do vision.

**Addendum slices**

6. The clip-length probe and the sweep on framework: findings cells.
7. The row's settings and reasons; redeploy on framework, probe.

## Decisions

- plan: The `architecture` overlap with v1.ai.processing and harness.optimization stays, as the
  intake accepted (PR #174 decision 2).
- secrets: Enrolled the laptop and gb10-2, gb10-2 in ai-hosting alone; enrolling gb10-1, the
  Framework, and framework-2 is left to a later task.
- hosts plan: Shell tests are plain-bash scripts run by the check; bats-core rejected: it adds a
  tool, and claude-plugins' plain-bash tests are the workspace precedent.
- migrate plan: the laptop's `~/experiments` clones stay. "Workstation" means the hosts (four, with framework-2).
- embedding plan: architecture never names the embedding model, the layout, or its rate.
- embedding (architect, at BRIEF): Embeddings have no cloud fallback; they route only to
  self-hosted rows. Rejected: e5 on Foundry managed compute (always on, about $384 a month, no
  scale to zero) and a closed serverless model (OpenAI, Cohere: different vectors, documents leave
  the premises).
- embedding plan: if e5 must be replaced, EmbeddingGemma 2 is the next candidate, if vLLM v0.34.0
  serves it. Rejected (framework-2, round R1): multilingual-e5-large-instruct, a variant of e5;
  the fallback is a distinct model.
- framework-2 plan (architect, round 2): no model of Chinese origin is admitted, and a fine-tune
  takes its base's origin; this replaces `ai-strategy.md`'s "only when no alternative serves".
- framework-2 (escalation 2): one port per alias stays; rejected every row on 8000 (no two rows
  could share a host).
- gateway plan (architect, round 1): the off-LAN path is removed from the goal: no peer relay, no
  relay-sized client timeouts, and no off-LAN cells. Rejected: keeping it in `gateway`, a `remote`
  task, or a backlog entry. This supersedes framework-2 (escalation 4)'s off-LAN line.
- gateway (escalation 2): `acceptance` proves bulk priority 10 and the 600 and 1,200 a-minute
  chat buckets live, with the bulk key it issues.
- vision-image-tokens (architect, at BRIEF): only models that incur cost are budgeted; no key's
  budget counts self-hosted rows. A new task `budgets`, before `acceptance`, builds it; until then
  bulk keeps its 1,000,000-token Block. This replaces `acceptance` proving the bulk Block's 429.
- vision-image-tokens (architect, redirect): the vision row's image budget is set for accuracy
  first, 1120 soft tokens, Gemma 4's largest; its cap is its throughput peak, images processed over
  time, not the most streams under a 10 s latency median; a request's latency only sizes client
  timeouts.
- vision-image-tokens: `acceptance` re-measures the vision row at 1120 in the standing layout cell
  with docinfer's dense pages, whose replies run to about 2,048 tokens against the bench's 100, and
  resets its cap and queue limit to that run's throughput peak. It moves `measured_vllm` to v0.34.0
  once it re-measures the fraction there; until then v0.33.0 stays, since `fraction_reason` rests on
  the spike's v0.33.0 E.1 cell.
- vision-image-tokens (escalation 2): a queue limit is any whole number of at least the cap, with
  `queue_limit_reason` when it isn't twice the cap; vision keeps 32, so bulk bursts wait for the
  local model instead of overflowing to Azure's different model.
