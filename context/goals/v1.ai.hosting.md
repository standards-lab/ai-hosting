## Task brief · framework-2

**Problem**

Embedding shares `framework` with audio, held to 14 req/s so audio keeps its RTFx. framework-2, a
second Strix Halo, is on the tailnet running Omarchy with only `~/ai-hosting` cloned. Moving
embedding there lets audio run alone at a higher cap and lets embedding run to its own ceiling.
Both Strix Halo hosts also keep their model store on the LUKS root while a second NVMe sits
unused, and the strix-halo runbook misses two package installs and runs `yq` before `mise
install`. With no cloud fallback for embeddings, the model choice is reopened against the
published multilingual retrieval scores, under an origin rule that now admits no Chinese-origin
model at all. `gateway`, `upgrades` and `acceptance` build on the layout this task leaves.

**Behaviors**
1. ai-hosting's `mise run currency` exits 0 or reports only held items: claude-plugins is at
   0.24.0, and the Python 3.15 hold in the bench environment has a Decisions line.
2. The strix-halo runbook, followed from a fresh Omarchy install, brings a host to serving: it
   installs `amd-debug-tools` and `amdgpu_top`, runs `mise install` before any step that uses
   `mise exec`, and its model-store step formats the host's second NVMe as plain ext4, adds an
   fstab line with `noatime`, and mounts it at `/srv/models` before the store is created.
3. framework-2 is set up by that runbook: Docker, `ip_nonlocal_bind`, the TTM limit, ufw, the
   store on its second disk, LUKS unlocked at the console, the suite linked, and the embedding row
   (`intfloat/multilingual-e5-large`) installed and healthy on port 8003. `hosting engine status`
   is clean, `hosting engine update` is a no-op, and its install record holds `embedding` only.
4. `framework` serves audio alone: embedding is uninstalled and its model pruned, its store has
   moved onto its second disk, mounted at `/srv/models`, and the audio row runs at cap 8,
   `queue_limit` 16, fraction 0.18, healthy, with `engine status` clean and `engine update` a
   no-op.
5. The bench gains an audio scenario at 8 streams with a data source long enough to run its full
   duration, and an embedding rate-sweep scenario on GuideLLM's built-in `sweep` profile;
   `levels.jq` names the sweep's synchronous and throughput levels; the bench lint and tests
   cover both scenarios.
6. The findings record, each with its command, time, path (`tailscale ping`), figures, and tool
   versions, run from the laptop: audio alone on `framework` at 8 streams (errors, preemption,
   RTFx median); embedding alone on framework-2, the cap-32 stream sweep and the rate sweep. The
   embedding limit N is the highest rate the row keeps with no errors and at least 98% of the
   target rate achieved, with no margin.
7. The strix-halo profile's audio reasons cite the 8-stream cell instead of the 14 req/s pair, and
   the embedding row's load target is "16 streams, at most N requests a second", citing the rate
   sweep. No document in ai-hosting still states the 14 req/s limit or places embedding on
   `framework`.
8. The findings record the embedding model comparison: the published BelebeleRetrieval nDCG@10
   over ~/docs' 37 subsets for the candidates, those excluded on origin or licence, and why e5
   stays (highest mean and lowest subset of any admitted model under 1B; the larger admitted one
   very likely misses the throughput floor).
9. ai-hosting's notes, runbooks, bench README and gateway note describe the final layout: agent
   on gb10-1, vision on gb10-2, audio alone on `framework`, embedding alone on framework-2, each
   Strix Halo store on its second disk, with the gateway's embedding bucket at N.
10. The goal record's Pending edits carry one coordinator edit for the final layout (replacing
    the `embedding` task's 14 req/s wording), an edit to `context/ai-strategy.md` making the
    origin rule absolute (no Chinese-origin model is admitted, fine-tunes taking their base's
    origin), and the record's embedding fallback decision drops its Chinese-origin candidate.

**Out of scope**

Enrolling framework-2 (or `framework`, gb10-1) in `fnox.toml`. Changing the embedding model.
Re-scoring retrieval (checkpoint and image are unchanged). Applying coordinator, go-ai or docinfer
edits, which sync applies; switching callers' `AI_EMBEDDING_BASE_URL` to framework-2:8003 beyond
saying so in the notes. Judging audio's RTFx floor of 5 on a direct path, which `acceptance`
does. Encrypting the store disks. The gateway.

**Door**

Two-way. Formatting framework-2's blank second disk and reformatting nothing else is the only
destructive step, on a disk with no data; `framework`'s store copy keeps the source until the row
runs healthy from the new disk. No release.

## Decisions

- plan: The `architecture` overlap with v1.ai.processing and harness.optimization stays, as the intake accepted (PR #174 decision 2).
- secrets plan: The gateway task replaces the spike gateway's key, `AI_GATEWAY_TOKEN` in go-ai's and
  docinfer's `fnox.toml`, with keys it issues.
- secrets: Enrolled the laptop and gb10-2, gb10-2 in ai-hosting alone; enrolling gb10-1, the
  Framework, and framework-2 is left to a later task.
- framework-2 (escalation 2): both Strix Halo hosts unlock LUKS at the console or remotely, through
  dropbear in the initramfs, reached on the LAN through a host that is up. Replaces the
  console-only decisions. Rejected: TPM2 auto-unlock (a stolen host unlocks itself, needs
  systemd hooks), Tailscale in the initramfs (its auth key unencrypted on `/boot`).
- framework-2 (escalation 2): one port per alias stays; rejected every row on 8000 (no two rows
  could share a host).
- hosts plan: Shell tests are plain-bash scripts run by the check; bats-core rejected: it adds a
  tool, and claude-plugins' plain-bash tests are the workspace precedent.
- migrate plan: the spike gateway is retired in migrate. Callers use the rows directly until the `gateway` task, whose handover starts from an empty gb10-2. Rejected: folding in `gateway`, which has six open points and depends on `embedding`; and a temporary gateway.
- migrate plan: `cleanup` is folded into migrate. It stays in `order` and Tasks, and is checked off at migrate's SHIP.
- migrate plan: the laptop's `~/experiments` clones stay. "Workstation" means the three hosts.

- embedding plan: architecture never names the embedding model, the layout, or its rate.
- embedding (architect, at BRIEF): Embeddings have no cloud fallback; they route only to
  self-hosted rows. Rejected: e5 on Foundry managed compute (always on, about $384 a month, no
  scale to zero) and a closed serverless model (OpenAI, Cohere: different vectors, documents leave
  the premises).
- embedding plan: Fallback order for the embedding model, unused since e5 passed:
  multilingual-e5-large, then EmbeddingGemma 2 (if vLLM v0.34.0 serves it),
  then a Chinese-origin candidate with its reason recorded. snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0 is
  excluded on origin: it is built on BAAI's bge-m3-retromae.

- framework-2 plan: a task in this goal before `gateway`, not a separate goal: the work is in this
  root, and `gateway`, `upgrades` and `acceptance` then build on the final layout. Rejected: a
  planned goal (it stages only after sync, and three tasks would be redone) and a task after
  `gateway`.
- framework-2 plan: audio runs alone on `framework` at cap 8, fraction 0.18; embedding moves to
  framework-2 and is re-measured alone, with no audio-protection rate limit.

- framework-2 plan: Chinese-origin models are not admitted at all (architect, round 2),
  replacing `ai-strategy.md`'s "only when no alternative serves".
- framework-2 plan: e5 stays the embedding model; the comparison lives in the findings.
- framework-2 plan: each Strix Halo store is its second NVMe, plain ext4 `noatime`, mounted at
  `/srv/models`; not encrypted: public weights (`development-secrets.md`).
- framework-2 plan: embedding's limit is the highest kept rate, no margin; cells run from the
  laptop; the rate sweep uses GuideLLM's `sweep` profile.
- framework-2: held python at 3.14.10 in the bench environment: 3.15.2 is out, but MTEB 2.27.22
  needs <3.15 and torchcodec has no 3.15 wheel.
